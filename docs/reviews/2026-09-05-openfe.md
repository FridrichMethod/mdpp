# OpenFE scientific review (2026-09-05)

Baseline: `eecb097`. Reviewed both notebooks in `examples/openfe/`, all 12
SMILES entries and their 24 corresponding open/closed complex PDBs, ligand
preparation, and the OpenFE submission/status/monitor scripts. The other PDBs
in the directory are unused by the current SMILES table. Priority was ordinary
scientific correctness and reproducible campaigns, rather than unusual inputs.

## Findings and fixes

| ID | Severity / classification | Evidence and impact | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OF-1 | HIGH, confirmed chemistry bug | `assign_topology()` called RDKit `AssignBondOrdersFromTemplate`, which did not retain ACCO_AMP's specified cyclooctene Z stereochemistry. Both PDB inputs have the correct Z geometry, but the returned molecular graph lacked the SMILES `/` and `\` bond stereo. Downstream chemistry/charging could reject the undefined stereochemistry or treat an incompletely specified isomer. | Validate input 3D stereochemistry against the template, reconstruct the complete template graph with the original heavy-atom ordering/coordinates and PDB atom identifiers, then add H. | OF-2 | HIGH, confirmed stale-input bug | Both notebooks used the same `tmp/` workspace. Charged SDF reuse checked only ligand count; GraphML reuse checked only file existence. | OF-3 | MEDIUM, scientific interpretation | The paired notebook equated matching inputs with directly comparable conformation-specific affinities, claimed higher asphericity meant better resolved poses, and claimed model-pKa preparation guaranteed identical protonation/net charge. ## Scientific checks without a confirmed bug

- **Ligand identity:** all 12 requested chemical species have formal charge -1.
  After OF-1, all 24 ligand/conformation inputs reproduce the exact canonical
  isomeric SMILES of their templates. Consequently the present transformations
  have zero formal-charge change; no charge-changing correction was arbitrarily
  added. This conclusion must be revisited if the ligand table changes.
- **Rigid alignment:** MDAnalysis `alignto` transforms the complete mobile
  universe by default even when given backbone AtomGroups; the single-state
  notebook does not leave its ligand behind. The paired SVD alignment uses a
  proper rotation and transforms the entire mobile complex.
- **Shared-pose geometry:** reproducing the paired selection/alignment on the
  bundled inputs selects BCN_AMP as both template proteins. Across the 24
  canonical-pose/target-protein combinations, minimum protein–ligand heavy-atom
  distances were 2.409–2.759 Å; no contact was shorter than 1.5 Å. Thus no gross
  shared-pose steric clash was confirmed. These distances do not establish a
  correct binding mode or adequate conformational sampling.
- **Thermodynamic cycle:** the hybrid protocol correctly builds separate
  A→B complex and solvent legs. The binding edge is
  `delta_conf = DG_complex,conf(A→B) - DG_solvent(A→B)`; negative favors B.
  SepTop correctly uses `mapping=None` and one transformation per ligand pair;
  its DAG contains separate solvent and complex simulations, and its final
  result already represents the binding edge including restraint accounting.
- **Paired solvent/gauge:** open and closed use identical solvent ChemicalSystems,
  mappings and protocol, hence the same deterministic GUFE solvent transformation
  identity. They are exported to distinct campaign parents. Executing both
  independently gives independent solvent estimates; actually reusing one
  result means it cancels exactly in `delta_open - delta_closed`, and its
  covariance must not be counted twice. Do not gather both receptor networks
  together just because ligand names coincide. Relative network energies have
  independent arbitrary offsets; the difference of matched ligand edges is
  identifiable, but a single ligand's absolute open/closed preference needs an
  external compatible reference offset. Apo populations enter overall binding.
- **Protocols and execution:** settings retain explicit 298.15 K, pH 7, 20 ns
  production, 51 hybrid lambda windows/replicas, and 1.0/1.5 nm complex/solvent
  padding. Charged ligands are reused consistently across legs. Resume paths
  retain checkpoints and array repeats have distinct working paths. The actual
  GPU script requests shared compute mode and intentionally does not start
  competing per-job MPS daemons; older AGENTS prose describing MPS is stale.
  No production scheduler calls or notifications were sent during this review.

## Literature and official implementation checks

1. [RDKit AssignBondOrdersFromTemplate](https://rdkit.org/docs/source/rdkit.Chem.AllChem.html)
   documents bond-order transfer; [RDKit stereochemistry operations](https://www.rdkit.org/docs/source/rdkit.Chem.rdmolops.html)
   documents coordinate-derived atom/bond stereo and atom reordering. The ACCO
   failure and its resolution were established on local input data, not inferred
   solely from documentation.
1. [Mey et al., Best Practices for Alchemical Free Energy Calculations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8388617/)
   motivates chemically correct inputs, experimental microstate/construct
   matching, and careful sampling. The notebook's input heuristics do not by
   themselves establish an experimentally appropriate ensemble.
1. [Klimovich, Shirts and Mobley, Guidelines for the analysis of free energy calculations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4420631/)
   supports overlap, decorrelation and convergence analysis. The
   [OpenFE hybrid-protocol guide](https://docs.openfree.energy/en/latest/guide/protocols/relativehybridtopology.html)
   provides the concrete overlap, mixing, forward/reverse and structural
   diagnostics and notes that initial structures should be stable under target
   conditions. No fixed runtime/window count guarantees these properties.
1. [OpenFE parallel quickrun execution](https://docs.openfree.energy/en/v1.9.1/guide/execution/quickrun_execution.html)
   describes distinct work/output paths for independent repeats.
   [Working with individual results](https://docs.openfree.energy/en/latest/guide/results/working_with_results.html)
   describes protocol aggregation and result estimates/uncertainties.
1. [OpenFE SepTop guide](https://docs.openfree.energy/en/latest/guide/protocols/septop.html)
   distinguishes separate topologies, automatic orientational restraints and
   the two-leg result. These ligand restraints are not receptor basin restraints.
1. [MDAnalysis alignment API](https://docs.mdanalysis.org/stable/documentation_pages/analysis/align.html)
   establishes the full-universe default transformation behavior.
1. [Mobley, Chodera and Dill, Confine-and-Release](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2562444/)
   explains the importance of protein conformational restriction and its
   free-energy contribution. No such corrections or apo population estimates
   can be inferred from the two initial protein structures alone.

Installed OpenFE/GUFE sources were additionally inspected for protocol defaults,
unit-aware settings serialization, deterministic object identity and SepTop
DAG construction. The `mdpp` environment does not include OpenFE/GUFE/OpenFF,
so OpenFE production/charging integration was not executed under that
environment and no dependencies were changed.

## Limits and follow-up science

No new RBFE simulations, AM1-BCC quantum calculations, or cluster jobs were run.
The source-only notebooks do not provide production trajectories/result JSONs,
so receptor basin occupancy, MBAR overlap, cycle closure, sampling adequacy and
real uncertainty cannot be established. The existing acyl-adenylate models lack
an experimentally justified treatment of potential catalytic metal/cofactor or
conserved-water effects; deciding their inclusion requires the intended LplA
construct, microstate and assay context, not guessed additions. Pocket protonation,
alternative poses and force-field accuracy for these flexible ligands remain
physical-model questions. The 1.0 nm complex padding is a computational choice:
check periodic image contacts if large domain excursions occur.

OpenFE also documents an upstream hybrid dummy-bond separability limitation.
This is not a newly established error in these particular mappings; difficult
edges should be inspected, and SepTop provides a different alchemical model.
Comparing OpenFE and FEP+ changes force fields/preparation/protocols, so any
observed difference cannot automatically be attributed to the simulation engine.
