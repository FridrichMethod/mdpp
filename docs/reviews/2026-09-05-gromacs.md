# GROMACS science and code review — 2026-09-05

Scope: all nine `examples/gromacs` notebooks; their trajectory, metric, contact, H-bond, torsion, PCA/TICA, FES and clustering implementations; related GROMACS preprocessing/analysis scripts. Review baseline: `eecb097`. Sources were checked online against primary/official documentation. Synthetic and command-stub verification is distinguished from real MD validation below.

## Findings and fixes

| ID | Severity | Before and scientific consequence | Change / regression evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | HIGH | Ramachandran flattened phi/psi columns by ordinal position. In a normal chain phi starts at residue 1 while psi starts at residue 0, pairing different residues. | Torsion labels retain original zero-based topology residue identity; plotting intersects phi/psi residue identities. Tests cover termini, multiple chains with repeated sequence numbering, atom slicing, unequal angle counts and no common residue. |
| G2 | HIGH | `compute_rmsd` called MDTraj on the caller trajectory, which centers coordinates in place. Later RMSF/DCCM could change merely because RMSD was computed first. | RMSD works on a selected coordinate copy and documents its own optimal fit. Regression checks exact coordinate preservation, RMSF invariance and zero RMSD under rigid motion. |
| G3 | HIGH | Requested H-bond cutoffs changed the presence matrix only after candidates had been filtered at MDTraj's default cutoffs and frequency. Relaxed bonds were missed; tightened cutoffs could retain bonds below `freq`. | Forward both geometric cutoffs to Baker-Hubbard candidate detection; use matching strict inequalities for presence. Regression tests include relaxed distance/angle and tightened occupancy filtering. |
| G4 | HIGH | Contact/H-bond/torsion examples applied minimum-image geometry after rotational fitting, while the cell retained its original orientation. Shell H-bond/SASA/DSSP analyses similarly consumed fitted coordinates. | Separate unfitted periodic coordinates from aligned fluctuation coordinates. Both postprocessors preserve an unfitted whole-solute artifact, used by those three shell analyses. Command-stub tests verify artifact retention and data flow. |
| G5 | HIGH | Structural examples fit raw periodic coordinates without making molecules whole, allowing artificial jumps/split molecules into RMSD, RMSF, Rg, SASA and DSSP. | Reconstruct bonded molecules before structural analysis; explicitly document that multimer/ligand assembly needs a deliberate imaging choice. No automatic assembly choice is claimed. |
| G6 | MEDIUM | `align=False` is no longer a valid RMSD/RMSF/DCCM keyword; three notebooks stopped with `TypeError`. Distance backend example compared periodic MDTraj with non-periodic Numba. Three plotting cells passed several axes to a one-axis `auto_ticks` API. | Remove obsolete arguments, iterate plot axes, and compare matching non-periodic observables. All nine templates now have executable regression coverage. |
| G7 | MEDIUM | Hierarchical accepted Ward/centroid/median on arbitrary pair-fitted RMSD matrices, despite their Euclidean-geometry requirement. | Reject unsupported methods before SciPy linkage, preserving average/complete/single/weighted. Tests reject all three invalid choices and verify weighted linkage. |
| G8 | MEDIUM, interpretation | Sin/cos PCA used z-scoring without stating the changed angular metric; H-bond “counts” excluded bonds below 10% occupancy; FES and other plots omitted critical ensemble/observable assumptions. | Examples use conventional unstandardized dPCA and unfiltered geometric H-bond counts; document ensemble temperature, reweighting limits, equilibration, retained-frame lag, shared projection axes/edges, correlated sampling and contact/Rg/SASA definitions. These are explicit best-practice choices, not claims that every alternative observable is invalid. |

The GROMOS representative field is also labeled accurately in the notebook: its greedy center need not be the distance-minimizing medoid. Centering on chain A does not guarantee the whole multimer is assembled; misleading script comments were corrected. Smoothed-coordinate output is explicitly visualization-only.

## Evidence and sources

1. [MDTraj phi API](https://mdtraj.readthedocs.io/en/latest/api/generated/mdtraj.compute_phi.html) returns the atom quadruples. The corresponding phi `C(i-1)-N(i)-CA(i)-C(i)` and psi `N(i)-CA(i)-C(i)-N(i+1)` central atoms identify residue i; tests verify mapping from the installed library.
1. [MDTraj RMSD API](https://mdtraj.readthedocs.io/en/latest/api/generated/mdtraj.rmsd.html) documents in-place centering and automatic optimal superposition.
1. [MDTraj Baker-Hubbard](https://www.mdtraj.org/1.9.7/api/generated/mdtraj.baker_hubbard.html) defines H–acceptor/angle cutoffs and frequency-filtered returned triplets. Installed MDTraj 1.11.1.post1 accepts both custom cutoff arguments.
1. [GROMACS terminology and preprocessing](https://manual.gromacs.org/current/user-guide/terminology.html#suggested-workflow) orders whole-molecule reconstruction before fitting and excludes subsequent PBC operations. [trjconv](https://manual.gromacs.org/current/onlinehelp/gmx-trjconv.html) distinguishes whole molecules, cluster imaging and nojump.
1. [SciPy linkage](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.cluster.hierarchy.linkage.html) states the Euclidean requirement for Ward, centroid and median.
1. [GROMACS dPCA](https://manual.gromacs.org/current/reference-manual/analysis/dihedral-pca.html) provides the sin/cos dihedral covariance workflow. The loss of circular metric under separate sine/cosine z-scoring follows directly from unequal axis scaling.
1. [Deeptime TICA](https://deeptime-ml.github.io/latest/api/generated/deeptime.decomposition.TICA.html) defines lagged covariance; its [trajectory dataset](https://deeptime-ml.github.io/latest/api/generated/deeptime.util.data.TrajectoryDataset.html) preserves independent trajectory boundaries. No concatenation bug exists in the supplied single-trajectory examples; the constraint is now explicit.
1. [Grossfield et al., sampling/uncertainty best practices](https://www.nist.gov/publications/best-practices-quantification-uncertainty-and-sampling-quality-molecular-simulations), DOI 10.33011/livecoms.1.1.5067, supports correlation-aware uncertainty and sampling assessment. [Shirts and Ferguson, free-energy surfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.01170) treats biased/multistate reweighting.
1. [MDTraj Rg](https://mdtraj.readthedocs.io/en/latest/api/generated/mdtraj.compute_rg.html) assumes equal masses without a mass array, whereas [GROMACS gyrate](https://manual.gromacs.org/current/onlinehelp/gmx-gyrate.html) defaults to mass weighting. The existing geometric Rg is retained and labeled rather than silently replaced.

## Audited areas without a general-use algorithm error

The FES histogram uses density normalization and correct `R T` units (kJ/mol), minimum shifting and masking; its plotting transpose agrees with histogram axis order. The exposed bins are uniform, so imshow's rectangular grid is appropriate. It has no reweighting API, which is now explicit. No bias correction was invented from missing metadata.

RMSF computes fluctuations around each atom's time mean; DCCM normalizes displacement covariance. Independent-replica delta-RMSF averages MSF, takes the square root and propagates SEM by the delta method, combining independent systems in quadrature. This is coherent for its documented estimand and independence assumptions; it is not the arithmetic mean of RMSFs or a confidence interval accounting for hidden slow states.

The PCA fit/project path reuses training centering/scaling and model transform correctly. TICA uses deeptime's lagged estimator and kinetic-map scaling. Sin/cos featurization preserves angular periodicity. Pair-fitted RMSD and clustering are separate from the representative display projection. Contact/native-Q cutoff calculations and DSSP frequency aggregation for complete protein backbones are internally consistent. Defaults for public compute backends were preserved.

The AMBER/CHARMM production templates retain force-field-specific cutoffs, HMR with constrained hydrogens, V-rescale and C-rescale. This inspection does not establish that a particular force field/system is correctly parameterized or that a 4 fs trajectory is physically converged; no MD rerun was performed.
